How it works

A better ear, one round at a time.

Every round is quick and repeatable — listen, answer, move on. Do enough of them and recognizing notes by ear turns from guesswork into instinct.

01 / LISTEN

Hear a note

Tap play and a single note — or a full chord — rings out on a real sampled piano. Replay it as many times as you need.

02 / GUESS

Find it on the keys

Press the key you think you heard. Name the chord, or play every note of it back. Instant right-or-wrong feedback, no guessing games about the score.

03 / LEARN

Build a streak

Track your best streak and accuracy as your ear sharpens. Widen the range from one octave to the full 88 keys when you're ready.
